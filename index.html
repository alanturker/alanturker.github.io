Common questions

I reinstalled and my progress is gone

Progress is stored on your device and is deleted along with the app, so a reinstall starts a fresh save. There is no account and no cloud copy, which also means nobody can see or restore your score.

Purchases are different: diamonds you bought and a TapAddict Pro subscription are tied to your Apple Account, not to the save. Open Settings inside the game and press Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought them on.

I paid but the diamonds did not arrive

Open the game and leave it open for a few seconds — an interrupted purchase is completed automatically the next time the app runs. If it still has not landed, press Restore Purchases in Settings. If that does not fix it, email us with the receipt Apple sent you and we will sort it out.

How do I cancel TapAddict Pro?

Inside the game: Profile → Settings → Manage TapAddict Pro. That opens Apple's own subscription screen, where you can switch between monthly and yearly or cancel. You can also reach it from the iOS Settings app by tapping your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling keeps Pro active until the end of the term you already paid for.

Can I turn off the reminders?

Profile → Settings → Reminders. They are capped at two a day and only ever fire when something you have built is about to be lost. If you previously told iOS not to allow notifications, that switch lives in the iOS Settings app under TapAddict and is the only one that can turn them back on.

How do I change my ad privacy choice?

Profile → Settings → Ad privacy options. That row appears if you are somewhere the choice applies, such as the EEA or the UK, and it reopens the consent form so you can change or withdraw what you agreed to.

Are there forced ads?

No. There are no banners, no pop-ups and nothing that interrupts a run. The only ad in the game is an optional rewarded video behind a button that tells you exactly what it pays before you press it.

Does buying anything raise my score?

No, and this is deliberate. Real money buys diamonds, which unlock different buttons, and Pro, which buys history, monthly diamonds and cosmetics. Nothing sold in the game increases your earn rate or your place on any board.
