Privacy Policy

Jelly Mix Jam · Last updated 24 October 2026

Jelly Mix Jam is a sliding jelly puzzle game made by Türker Alan. This page is the privacy notice for the iOS and Android apps. In short: the game has no account and no server of its own. Your progress stays on your device. We show ads through Google AdMob (with Unity Ads as an ad partner) under your consent choices, and in-app purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play.

Who we are

The controller for Jelly Mix Jam is Türker Alan. Questions: alanturker@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

  • Progress. Your current level, coins, boosters, win streaks, simple play statistics (for example levels cleared, cuts and mixes used) and which tutorials you have seen. Used to continue where you left off and for achievements.
  • Purchases. Whether "Remove Ads" has been bought.
  • Settings. Sound and vibration preferences.

This data is kept in the app's local storage. We do not upload it, and we cannot see it.

Ads (Google AdMob and Unity Ads)

  • Jelly Mix Jam shows optional rewarded ads (for example extra time or double coins) and interstitial ads between some levels. The first levels are ad-free. Buying "Remove Ads" turns interstitial ads off.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. Through AdMob mediation, Unity Ads may also bid to show an ad. To serve and measure ads, these companies may collect device and usage data such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, coarse location derived from it, device model, OS version and ad interactions. Their policies apply: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses data from partner apps, Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Consent. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions that require it, we show Google's consent form (UMP, IAB TCF) before ads are personalised. You can open the form again from the game's Settings.
  • iOS tracking. iOS may ask whether Jelly Mix Jam can track you across apps (App Tracking Transparency). If you refuse, ads are still shown but are not personalised with the IDFA.
  • Without an internet connection no ads are requested.

In-app purchases

Purchases ("Remove Ads", booster packs) are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details; the store only tells the app which product was bought so it can be delivered.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, precise location or health data. There are no accounts, chats or leaderboards, and the game has no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own.

Retention and deletion

Game data stays on the device until you delete the app. Deleting the app deletes it (a "Remove Ads" purchase can be restored from the store). Data processed by Google and Unity for ads follows their own retention rules; you can reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Because we hold no personal data on servers, most requests are met by deleting the app or changing your ad choices. For anything else, email us.

Children

Jelly Mix Jam is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher digital-consent age in your country).

International transfers

Google, Unity and their ad partners may process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own terms and transfer safeguards.

Changes

If we add something new we will update this page, the date above, and the store privacy labels.

Biz kimiz

Oyunun cihazında sakladıkları

Reklamlar (Google AdMob ve Unity Ads)

  • Jelly Mix Jam isteğe bağlı ödüllü reklamlar (örneğin ek süre veya iki kat coin) ve bazı level'lar arasında geçiş reklamları gösterir. İlk level'lar reklamsızdır. "Reklamları kaldır" satın alınırsa geçiş reklamları kapanır.
  • Reklamları Google AdMob sunar. AdMob uyumlulaştırması üzerinden Unity Ads de reklam göstermek için teklif verebilir. Reklam sunmak ve ölçmek için bu şirketler reklam kimliği (iOS'ta IDFA, Android'de Reklam Kimliği), IP adresi ve buna dayalı yaklaşık konum, cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi sürümü ve reklam etkileşimleri gibi verileri toplayabilir. Kendi politikaları geçerlidir: Google Gizlilik Politikası, Google'ın iş ortağı uygulamalardan gelen verileri nasıl kullandığı, Unity Gizlilik Politikası.
  • Onay. AEA, Birleşik Krallık, İsviçre ve gerektiren diğer bölgelerde reklamlar kişiselleştirilmeden önce Google'ın onay formunu (UMP, IAB TCF) gösteririz. Formu oyundaki Ayarlar'dan yeniden açabilirsin.
  • iOS izleme. iOS, Jelly Mix Jam'in seni uygulamalar arasında izleyip izleyemeyeceğini sorabilir (App Tracking Transparency). Reddedersen reklamlar yine gösterilir ama IDFA ile kişiselleştirilmez.
  • İnternet bağlantısı yokken reklam istenmez.
