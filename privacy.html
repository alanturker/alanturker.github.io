TapAddict Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

In short: TapAddict has no accounts and no servers. Your progress is stored on your device and nothing you do in the game is sent to us. The only company that receives any data is Google, and only when you choose to watch an optional rewarded video.

Who this policy is from

TapAddict is an iOS game published by Turker Alan. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it. If anything here is unclear, the contact address at the bottom reaches a person.

We do not ask you to create an account

There is no sign-in, no email address, no password and no profile held anywhere. The name you type for the leaderboard is stored only on your device and is never uploaded.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps its state locally, in your app's private storage, where no other app can read it:

  • Your save file — score, level, buttons owned, upgrades, streak, badges, missions, season progress and settings.
  • Two items in the iOS Keychain: a key used to detect a tampered save file, and your highest recorded score.

All of this is removed when you delete the app. We have no copy of it, so we cannot restore it for you and we cannot see it.

What the game does not do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting service is built into the app.
  • No advertising identifier is requested for tracking. The app never shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt because it never tracks you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The leaderboard opponents are not real people. Every rival name and score shown is generated on your device from the current week, which is why the board works with no internet connection and why no score of yours is ever submitted anywhere.

Advertising

TapAddict shows no banners, no pop-ups and no unskippable ads. The only advertising in the game is an optional rewarded video that plays after you press a button telling you what it pays. If you never press it, no ad is ever requested and no ad request is made in the background.

These videos are served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure them, Google may receive information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and operating system version, and information about the ad itself. Google acts as an independent controller of that data. What it collects and how it uses it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before any ad is requested, using Google's consent form. You can change or withdraw that consent at any time from the game's Settings screen, under "Ad privacy options". Ads shown without consent are non-personalised: they are chosen from general context rather than from any profile of you.

Purchases

Diamond packs and the TapAddict Pro subscription are sold through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Payment is handled entirely by Apple — we never see your card details, billing address or Apple Account. The app receives only a confirmation that a purchase completed, which it uses to add the diamonds or unlock Pro. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions renew through your Apple Account and can be cancelled at any time from Settings on your device, or from the "Manage TapAddict Pro" link inside the game.

Notifications

If you allow reminders, they are scheduled by the app on your own device — for example, an evening reminder when a streak you have built is still unplayed. There is no push server involved, so no message is ever sent from us to your phone and nothing about your habits leaves the device to generate one. Reminders are capped at two a day and can be switched off in the game's Settings, or in the iOS Settings app.

Sharing your score

The share card is drawn on your device as an image. When you send it, iOS hands that image to whichever app you choose — Messages, Instagram or anything else. We receive nothing in the process, and once the image is in another app, that app's own privacy policy applies to it.

Children

TapAddict is not directed at children under 13 and is not registered as a child-directed app with any ad network. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, please contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or delete — deleting the app removes everything the game stored. For the advertising data described above, your rights are exercised against Google: withdraw consent from the game's Settings, and use Google's own ad settings to control personalisation across Google services. You may also reset or limit your device's advertising identifier in the iOS Settings app, under Privacy & Security.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts doing something this page does not describe — an online leaderboard would be the likely reason — this policy is updated before that version ships, and the date at the top changes with it.
