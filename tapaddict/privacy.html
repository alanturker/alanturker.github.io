Privacy Policy

TapAddict · Last updated 9 September 2026

TapAddict is a tap arcade made by Türker Alan. This page is the privacy notice for the iOS and Android apps. It covers what we store, what Google and Apple see, and how you can ask us to delete an account.

Who we are

The controller for TapAddict is Türker Alan. Game servers run on Google Firebase in the europe-west1 region (Belgium). Questions: alanturker@gmail.com.

What we collect

We collect only what the game needs to run, rank, match, restore, and stay funded.

  • Account id. On first launch we create an anonymous Firebase Auth user so a score, a friend list and a cloud save have somewhere to live. You may later link Sign in with Apple or Google. Apple may give us a name if you allow that scope. Google / Apple may give Firebase an email address on the auth record. We do not use that email in the game UI, and we do not sell it.
  • Username and friend code. A public handle (3–16 characters) and a six-character friend code. Other signed-in players can search by name prefix or exact code. Your profile document (username, level, friend code, account timestamps) is readable by other signed-in players so boards, friends and duel results can show who they are looking at.
  • Gameplay and progress. Score, level, upgrades, buttons, badges, missions, streaks, encounter progress, duel record, ad-watch ledger and similar save fields. A copy lives on the device. A second copy is written to Firestore under your account so losing the phone is not losing the run. Only you can read or write that save.
  • Leaderboards. When you have a username, we submit score, level and name to weekly, monthly and all-time boards. Signed-in players can read those rows.
  • Friends and requests. Who you added, who asked, and the username / level shown on those rows. Friend lists are owner-only. We do not read your phone contacts.
  • Duels. Room membership, alias, stake, live score ticks, invite codes, and friend-call invites (room id, who called, stake). Live rooms sit in Firebase Realtime Database. Finished matches may be archived on the server for settlement; the client learns results from the live room and from its own save.
  • Purchases. Apple and Google process payments. We store entitlement and a local purchase ledger so Pro and diamond packs restore. We do not see your full card number.
  • Ads. Optional rewarded ads via Google AdMob. In the EEA, UK and other regulated regions we show Google’s UMP consent form before requesting an ad. iOS may ask for App Tracking Transparency so ads can be relevant; refusing does not change the game and rewarded ads still pay out. Android may use the advertising id when the platform allows it.
  • Analytics. Firebase Analytics is off until consent is resolved. Outside regions that require a form we treat analytics as allowed. Inside those regions we follow the form. Events are about play (level-ups, unlocks, spend of in-game currency, equipped button), not your real name.
  • Crashes. Firebase Crashlytics starts at launch so a boot crash can be fixed. Reports may include device/OS facts plus game keys we attach (level, equipped button, Pro, owned button count). They are not used to advertise.
  • Local settings. Theme, sound, haptics, locale, reminder toggle and similar preferences stay in app storage / UserDefaults.
  • Notifications. Optional local reminders (streak, league, missions, rest bonus). They are scheduled on the device. We do not run a push-notification server and we do not upload your notification permission.
  • Share posters. If you share a card, the image is rendered on the device and handed to the system share sheet. We do not upload that image to our servers.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your contacts, camera, microphone, photos library, precise location, or health data. We do not use the game as a social network beyond username, friend code, friend list and duel invites.

Why we use it

  • Run the game and restore progress across devices and platforms.
  • Show ranks, friends, duel lobbies and incoming friend calls.
  • Settle wagers and keep an escrow honest.
  • Unlock Pro and diamond packs you paid for.
  • Show optional rewarded ads and measure whether the game is healthy.
  • Fix crashes.

Who else sees data

  • Google Firebase — auth, database, functions, analytics, crash reports. Region: europe-west1 for functions and the project’s EU database configuration.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads — ad serving, consent (UMP / IAB TCF), and advertising identifiers when allowed. Google’s policies apply: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Apple — App Store, Sign in with Apple, StoreKit, and (if you allow tracking) the advertising identifier / SKAdNetwork.
  • Other players — username, level, board rows, and whatever a friend or duel opponent must see to play with you. They cannot read your save or your friend list.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your save to train unrelated products.

Retention

The local save stays until you delete the app or wipe the file. The cloud save, profile, friends, boards and duel artefacts stay while the account exists. Anonymous accounts that are never linked still occupy a Firebase uid for that install. Analytics and crash logs follow Google’s retention for those products. Live duel rooms and friend invites are short-lived operational data.

Your choices

  • Play without linking Apple or Google. Online features still use the anonymous account.
  • Skip claiming a username. Boards, friends and some online actions require one.
  • Refuse tracking on iOS. The game and rewarded ads still work.
  • In the EEA and UK, change ad choices from Settings when that row appears.
  • Turn reminders off in Settings, or in system settings.
  • Sign out of a linked account from the account screen.

Access, correction, deletion

You can change your username in-app (the previous name is released). To see or delete the server copy of an account, email alanturker@gmail.com with enough to find it (username, friend code, or the store account you linked). We will delete the Firebase profile, cloud save, friend edges, username lock, friend code and related board rows we control. Apple and Google keep their own payment and sign-in records. There is not yet a one-tap delete button in the app; a request to us is the path.

If you are in the EEA, UK or a similar regime you may also object, restrict, or complain to your data-protection authority. For Türkiye, you may apply to the KVKK.

Children

TapAddict is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher digital-consent age in your country). Do not create an account for a child with Apple or Google if they cannot consent.

International transfers

We host gameplay backends in the EU. Google, Apple and ad partners may process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own terms and transfer tools.

Security

Clients cannot write leaderboards, usernames, friendships or duel rooms directly; those go through Cloud Functions. Cloud saves are owner-only. That is not a promise that no one can ever break in. Do not put secrets in your username.

Changes

If we add a new collector or a new public surface (for example a new social feature), we will update this page and the date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the store privacy labels where the stores require it.
